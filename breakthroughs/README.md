# Breakthrough candidates

**No breakthrough is established by the completed studies.**

A candidate belongs here only with a precise new claim, strong and fairly matched baselines, useful factual fidelity or task performance, independent replication appropriate to that claim, a primary-source prior-art review, and reproducible code/data. Ordinary engineering improvements and negative findings remain in [the findings index](../discoveries/README.md). A promising result must retain its limitations and provisional status.
